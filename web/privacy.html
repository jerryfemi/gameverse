GameVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use GameVerse (DunkVerse) and link your Google Account, we receive your basic public profile information:

  • Your Google Display Name and Avatar (to display on player cards and leaderboards)
  • Your Email Address (to restore your progress, match trophies, and head-to-head records across devices)
  • In-game gameplay telemetry, scores, and match results

2. How We Use Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Save and sync your game progress across sessions and devices
  • Facilitate real-time 1v1 multiplayer matches and head-to-head records
  • Display friend activity and in-game challenges

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties or advertisers. All game synchronization is securely handled via Google Firebase Cloud services.

4. Account Deletion & Contact

You can sign out at any time from your in-game profile. If you wish to delete your account data, please contact the developer at woobackbigmlboa@gmail.com.
